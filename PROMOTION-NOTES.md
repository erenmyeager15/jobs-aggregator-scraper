## Short tutorial script

1. Open the Actor page.
2. Keep `source` set to `all`.
3. Enter one keyword, for example `software`.
4. Set `maxResults` to `1` for the smallest sample.
5. Run the Actor and open the Jobs dataset view.
6. Review the source URL and attribution, then export to CSV or connect the dataset to a workflow.

## Social post draft

I polished my Jobs Aggregator Scraper on Apify.

It combines public remote job listings from Remote OK, Remotive, and Arbeitnow in one deduplicated dataset. Search by role, skill, company, location, or date and export titles, companies, salary fields when available, source URLs, and attribution.

Useful for remote-hiring research, job-market monitoring, and internal data workflows. No source API key is required.

Feedback welcome on the README, sample input, and output fields.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim coverage beyond Remote OK, Remotive, and Arbeitnow.
- Do not promise that every listing includes salary, job type, tags, or descriptions.
- Do not promote the Actor for scraping personal profiles, resumes, emails, or phone numbers.
- Do not encourage spam, discriminatory profiling, or automated employment decisions.
- Do not describe Remotive results as safe for third-party job-board republishing.
- Keep source URLs and attribution visible in demos.
